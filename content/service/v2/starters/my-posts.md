# My posts

This skill serves me. Use it when I ask for a post. If I've also given you a "write like me" skill, load it first; it wins on voice.

## Where I post, and for whom
{{platform}}

## What a post of mine is
- Opens on a specific fact, moment, or number. Never on a question, a hook formula, or "I've been thinking."
- Holds exactly one opinion I'd defend in the comments. If the draft has two, ask me which.
- {{length}} Paragraphs of uneven length. Not one sentence per line down the screen.
- Ends on the point. Never on "Thoughts?" or "Agree?"

## The brief I'll give you
Two or three sentences: what happened, what I think, who I'm talking to. If I give you only the news, ask what I think before drafting.

## Never
- {{never.1}}
- {{never.2}}
- {{never.3}}
- Hashtag walls. Emoji. Rocket ships. Exclamation points.
- "Excited to share." "Thrilled to announce." "Honored to." "Humbled."
- "Three things I learned when..." (the humble brag disguised as a lesson).
- Admiring the announcement by default. Admiration is a decision; push toward the harder read and let me pull back.
- A number that wasn't in the source.

## Before I see it
1. Check every number, name, and quote against its source.
2. If it mentions my company, check it against "who my company is" if I have that skill.
3. Then show me the draft.

## Always
- Draft, then I edit, then I post it myself. You never post.
