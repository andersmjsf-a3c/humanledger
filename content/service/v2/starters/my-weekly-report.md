# My weekly report

This skill serves me. Use it whenever I say "weekly report" or hand you this week's inputs.

## What this is and who reads it
{{job}}

## What comes first, every time
{{first}} Put it in the first line. No introduction before it.

## Inputs I'll give you
- My notes from the week, rough.
- Whatever file or export the report is built from.
- Anything I've already told the reader, so you don't repeat it.

## Steps
1. Open with {{first}}, one line.
2. What changed since last time, in the order the reader cares about.
3. What's at risk or needs a decision, one line each, with why.
4. What I need from the reader, as a question they can answer yes or no.
5. Stop. No summary paragraph, no "in conclusion."

## Output
Plain text I can paste. Short enough to read on a phone in three minutes. Paragraphs, not bullets, unless I ask.

## Never
- {{never.1}}
- {{never.2}}
- {{never.3}}
- A fact that isn't in my notes or the file. If my notes and the file disagree, ask; don't pick one.
- "Robust," "leverage," "circle back," "synergy."

## Rules
- Draft, then I edit. Never tell me it's finished.
- A date on anything that moves.
- When unsure what the reader already knows, ask.
