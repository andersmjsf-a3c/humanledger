# Homepage copy (Oct 26 2026 mockup)

**Eyebrow:** For anyone using AI at work

**H1:** AI changes every week. What you know about it shouldn't have to start over.

**Sub:** The names move, the settings move, the model you were talking to gets replaced. Humanledger makes it understandable in plain words, keeps what you learn organized in one place that's yours, and gives you tools that make it work better for you. No course. No signup to start.

**Buttons:** Start with what you're trying to do · See what changed this week

## What are you trying to do?
Pick the one closest. Each opens a short page: the rule in a paragraph, something to paste into your AI right now, and an example.

1. Write something that sounds like me (Voice, drafts, corrections)
2. Check whether something it told me is true (Sources, dates, the fact pass)
3. Teach it a job I do every week (Skills, written once)
4. Find out something I can rely on (Research with a trail)
5. Make a deck or a one-pager someone will judge (Briefs, standards, passes)
6. Figure out what just changed (Releases, renamed things, settings)
7. Keep it from forgetting who I am (Memory, projects, context)
8. Understand a word everyone keeps using (The glossary, in plain words)

## Three things, in the order you'll want them
Most people never get past the first. That's fine. The second and third are there when a result is worth keeping.

**Understand. Plain words for all of it.** A glossary where every term gets one or two sentences a person can repeat. A page that says where each setting and feature is today, dated, and what it used to be called. A log of what changed and what to re-check. Free, no account.

**Organize. One place for what you've learned.** Your rules, your voice, your skills, your corrections, kept as files you own, versioned, and ready to load into your AI. The service never reads them. It keeps score of how they're doing and tells you when one needs a re-test.

**Tools. Make it work better for you.** Turn a conversation where you corrected it three times into a skill it reads every time. Run a fact check before anything goes out. Connect it to your AI so a correction you make mid-conversation lands in the file, as a change you approve.

## Where things live
As of today. This page will be wrong first, so we keep it current. The habits don't move. Only the buttons do. When a release lands, this page is what we update first, with the date.
Rows: Last checked · Turn memory on or off · Upload a skill · Web search toggle · Which model you're talking to · Names that no longer appear

## What changed
A new model landed. Here's what to re-check. Every release, in civilian terms: what's different, what it tends to break in the rules people keep, and the five-minute re-test to run on anything you rely on.

## A glossary you can repeat out loud
One or two sentences each. No jargon in the definition of jargon. (Three sample cards: Skill, Hallucination, Agent.)
