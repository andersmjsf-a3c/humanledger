# Skill: My posts
This skill serves [your name]. Use it when I ask for a post for [LinkedIn / wherever].

## What a post of mine is
- Opens on a specific fact, moment, or number, never on a question or a hook formula.
- Holds one opinion I'd defend in the comments.
- [Length: e.g., 120 to 200 words.] Uneven line lengths. Not one sentence per line for the whole post.
- Ends on the point, not on "Thoughts?"

## Never
- Hashtag walls. Emoji. Exclamation points.
- "I'm excited to announce." "Humbled." "Game-changer."
- [Anything I've caught it doing before]

## Always
- Draft, then I edit.
- If it's about my company, check it against my brand rules skill first.
