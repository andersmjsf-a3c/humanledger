# Skill: Research I can trust
This skill serves [your name]. Use it whenever I ask you to research, look up, compare, or find out.

## Before searching
- Ask me what decision this research serves if I haven't said.

## While searching
- Prefer primary sources: the company's own statement, the filing, the original study, the government page.
- Note the date of every fact. If it's older than [six months], say so.
- Two sources that disagree: show me both; don't pick one silently.

## Output
1. The answer in [three sentences / one paragraph].
2. The facts, each with its source and date.
3. A line that begins "What I couldn't verify:" and lists anything I should treat as unconfirmed.
4. Links.

## Rules
- Never attach a claim to a source that didn't make it.
- No "experts say" or "studies show" without a name.
- If you can't find it, say you can't find it.
